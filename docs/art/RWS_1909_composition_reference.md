# RWS 1909 composition reference (content checklist per card)

Left/right = viewer's left/right. Draw ONLY these elements (plus the ED-02 border). No text anywhere.

## Major Arcana
- 00 The Fool: young man in patterned tunic with feather cap, at cliff edge stepping right-to-left toward the edge, white rose in left hand (viewer's left), staff with small bag over right shoulder, small white dog leaping at his feet, white sun upper right, yellow sky, snowy mountains far behind.
- 01 The Magician: man in red robe over white tunic, right arm raises white double-ended wand, left hand points down, lemniscate above head, snake-belt biting tail; table in front with cup, sword, pentacle, wand; red roses arching above, roses and white lilies in foreground; yellow sky.
- 02 The High Priestess: seated woman in blue robe & white veil, horned-moon crown (full moon between horns), equal-armed cross on chest, half-hidden scroll in lap (no letters), crescent moon at feet; between black pillar (left) and white pillar (right) with lotus capitals (no letters); veil behind embroidered with pomegranates; water glimpsed behind.
- 03 The Empress: crowned woman with twelve stars, robe with pomegranate pattern, seated on cushions, sceptre in right hand, heart-shaped shield with Venus symbol beside her; ripe wheat field in foreground, forest of cypress trees and waterfall behind; yellow sky.
- 04 The Emperor: bearded old king in armour under red robe, on stone throne carved with four ram heads, ankh sceptre in right hand, orb in left; barren orange mountains, small river at their foot; orange sky.
- 05 The Hierophant: pope in red robes and triple crown, seated between two grey pillars, right hand blessing, left holds triple cross; two crossed keys at his feet; two tonsured monks kneeling below, one in robe with roses, one with lilies.
- 06 The Lovers: winged angel (Raphael) in purple robe above in clouds with large sun behind; draped man (left) before tree with flame-like leaves; draped woman (right) before tree of knowledge with serpent coiled around it; mountain peak between them.
- 07 The Chariot: armoured prince with star crown and crescent moons on shoulders, holding wand, standing in stone chariot under starry canopy; black sphinx and white sphinx lying in front; walled city and river behind; winged disc on chariot front.
- 08 Strength: woman in white with flowered belt and lemniscate above head, gently closing the jaws of a red-gold lion; garland of flowers looping to lion; blue mountain behind; yellow sky.
- 09 The Hermit: old bearded man in grey hooded cloak on snowy peak, head bowed, holding lantern with six-pointed star in right hand, tall staff in left; dark blue-grey sky.
- 10 Wheel of Fortune: large orange wheel in sky with ornamental marks (no letters) and alchemical-like symbols replaced by abstract ornament; sphinx with sword on top, snake descending on left, red jackal-headed Anubis rising on right; four winged creatures reading books in clouds at corners (angel top-left, eagle top-right, bull bottom-left, lion bottom-right); blue sky.
- 11 Justice: crowned figure in red robe and green cape, seated between two grey pillars with purple veil behind, raised upright sword in right hand (viewer's left), scales in left hand (viewer's right); yellow ground.
- 12 The Hanged Man: man hanging upside down by right foot from a T-shaped (tau) living wooden cross with leaves, left leg bent behind forming a figure 4, hands behind back, yellow halo around head, blue tunic, red tights; calm face; plain light background.
- 13 Death: skeleton in black armour on white horse, carrying black banner with white five-petalled rose; fallen king beneath horse, bishop in gold praying, maiden turned away, child offering flowers; river with boat, sun rising between two towers in distance. Symbolic, no gore.
- 14 Temperance: winged angel in white with triangle-in-square on chest, sun disc on brow, pouring water between two cups; one foot in pool, one on land; path leading to mountains with crown-like glow; irises beside pool.
- 15 The Devil: horned goat-headed devil with bat wings squatting on black block, inverted pentagram above brow, right hand raised, left holds downward torch; chained naked man and woman with small horns and tails below (draped/tasteful: loose cloth); black background.
- 16 The Tower: tall grey tower on rocky peak struck by lightning, crown knocked off top, flames from windows, two figures falling headfirst, falling yellow flame drops (yods); black sky.
- 17 The Star: kneeling woman (draped, tasteful) by pool, one foot on water, pouring water from two jugs—one into pool, one onto land in five streams; one large eight-pointed star with seven smaller white stars; ibis bird on tree at right; distant hills.
- 18 The Moon: full moon with crescent and face, dropping yods; dog (left) and wolf (right) howling; crayfish emerging from pool in foreground; path winding between two towers toward mountains.
- 19 The Sun: large sun with face and straight & wavy rays; naked child (tasteful) with feather on head riding white horse, holding red banner; wall of sunflowers behind.
- 20 Judgement: angel Gabriel in clouds blowing trumpet with banner bearing a cross; grey figures (man, woman, child) rising from coffins on water, arms raised; icy mountains behind.
- 21 The World: dancing figure wrapped in a violet sash holding two wands, inside large oval laurel wreath tied with red ribbons top and bottom; four corners: angel (top-left), eagle (top-right), bull (bottom-left), lion (bottom-right) in clouds; blue sky.

## Wands (wands are plain wooden staves sprouting a few green leaves)
- 22 Ace: hand emerging from a cloud holding sprouting wand, leaves falling, castle on hill bottom-left, river and trees.
- 23 Two: man in red/brown on battlement holds globe in right hand and wand in left; second wand fixed to wall on right; rose-and-lily cross emblem on wall at left; sea and coast beyond.
- 24 Three: man seen from behind on cliff, three wands planted, looking at ships on golden sea.
- 25 Four: four wands with garland of flowers and fruit; two women raising bouquets; castle and people behind; yellow sky.
- 26 Five: five youths sparring with wands, no injuries.
- 27 Six: laurel-crowned rider on white horse holding wand with wreath; five wands held by foot followers.
- 28 Seven: man on high ground defending with one wand against six wands rising from below; mismatched shoes.
- 29 Eight: eight wands flying diagonally through clear sky over river and green landscape; no figures.
- 30 Nine: wounded man with bandaged head leaning on wand, wary, eight wands upright behind him like a fence.
- 31 Ten: man struggling forward carrying ten wands bundled, towards a town/house.
- 32 Page: youth in feathered hat and salamander-patterned tunic holding tall wand, looking at it; desert with pyramids-like mounds.
- 33 Knight: armoured knight in salamander tunic on rearing brown horse, holding wand; desert and pyramids.
- 34 Queen: queen on throne with lion carvings, wand in right hand, sunflower in left, black cat at her feet; lions banner behind.
- 35 King: king on throne decorated with lions and salamanders, holding flowering wand; small salamander at his feet.

## Cups (golden goblets)
- 36 Ace: hand emerging from a cloud holding cup with W-shaped ornament replaced by abstract mark; five streams falling; dove with wafer (equal-armed cross) descending; pond with water lilies; falling droplets (yods). No sun.
- 37 Two: young man (left) and woman (right) exchanging cups; caduceus with winged red lion head above; house on hill behind. No sun, no pond.
- 38 Three: three maidens dancing in a circle raising cups; fruit and pumpkins harvest at feet.
- 39 Four: young man sitting under tree, arms crossed, looking at three cups on ground; hand from cloud offering fourth cup.
- 40 Five: cloaked figure in black head bowed, three spilled cups in front, two upright cups behind; river and bridge leading to castle.
- 41 Six: boy offering cup filled with white flowers to small girl; six cups with flowers; old village courtyard, guard walking away.
- 42 Seven: figure in silhouette before seven cups on clouds holding visions: head, veiled figure, snake, castle, jewels, laurel wreath, dragon.
- 43 Eight: cloaked man with staff walking away up rocky hills; eight cups stacked in front (5 below, 3 above, gap); moon with face (eclipse) above.
- 44 Nine: contented man seated on bench, arms crossed, nine cups arranged on draped arched shelf behind him.
- 45 Ten: couple embracing with raised arms, two children dancing; rainbow of ten cups arching in sky; house and river.
- 46 Page: youth in floral tunic holding cup with a fish peeking out; sea waves behind.
- 47 Knight: knight on calm white horse holding cup forward; winged helmet and boots; river and trees.
- 48 Queen: queen on throne at sea edge with cherubs, gazing at ornate lidded cup; pebbles and water at feet.
- 49 King: king on stone throne floating on rough sea, holding cup and short sceptre; fish pendant; ship and leaping fish behind.

## Swords (plain steel swords)
- 50 Ace: hand emerging from a cloud holding upright sword; golden crown encircling tip with olive branch (left) and palm frond (right) hanging; yods falling; grey mountains.
- 51 Two: blindfolded woman in white on stone bench, arms crossed holding two swords; crescent moon upper right; sea with rocks behind.
- 52 Three: red heart pierced by three swords; grey storm clouds and rain.
- 53 Four: knight effigy lying on tomb, hands in prayer; one sword carved on tomb side, three swords hanging on wall; stained-glass window upper left.
- 54 Five: smug man holding three swords, two swords on ground; two dejected figures walking away toward sea; jagged clouds.
- 55 Six: ferryman poling boat with cloaked woman and child; six swords upright in boat; calm water on right, choppy on left; far shore.
- 56 Seven: man tiptoeing away carrying five swords, two swords left planted behind; army tents in camp.
- 57 Eight: bound and blindfolded woman standing amid eight swords stuck in ground; castle on cliff behind; muddy water.
- 58 Nine: person sitting up in bed, face in hands; nine swords hung horizontally on black wall; quilt with roses and astrological squares (no glyph text).
- 59 Ten: man lying face down on shore, red cloth over lower body, ten swords pierce his back (no blood); black sky above, yellow dawn at horizon over calm water, distant mountains.
- 60 Page: youth holding upright sword with both hands, on windy hill, birds flying, clouds.
- 61 Knight: knight charging on white horse with sword raised, storm clouds, wind-blown trees, birds.
- 62 Queen: queen on throne carved with butterflies and cherub, sword held upright in right hand, left hand raised; clouds, single bird.
- 63 King: king seated frontally holding upright sword, butterfly-carved throne, trees and clouds.

## Pentacles (golden discs each with an engraved five-pointed star)
- 64 Ace: hand emerging from a cloud holding large pentacle (palm up); garden with white lilies, hedge with archway opening to mountains beyond.
- 65 Two: young man in tall hat juggling two pentacles linked by a green infinity loop; ships on high waves behind.
- 66 Three: stonemason on bench working in cathedral arch; monk and architect with plans watching; three pentacles carved in the arch.
- 67 Four: crowned man seated clutching one pentacle, one on crown, two under feet; city behind.
- 68 Five: two beggars in snow (one on crutches, one shawled) passing lit stained-glass church window with five pentacles. Tasteful.
- 69 Six: merchant with scales giving coins to two kneeling beggars; six pentacles in air.
- 70 Seven: young farmer leaning on hoe looking at seven pentacles growing on green bush.
- 71 Eight: craftsman at bench carving pentacles; six displayed on post, one on ground, one in hand; town in distance.
- 72 Nine: woman in robe with sunflowers pattern in vineyard, hooded falcon on gloved hand, nine pentacles on vines; manor house behind.
- 73 Ten: old man in robe with grapes pattern seated with two dogs; couple and child under archway; ten pentacles arranged in Tree-of-Life pattern overlay; castle courtyard.
- 74 Page: youth in green field holding pentacle up gazing at it; ploughed field, trees, mountains.
- 75 Knight: knight on still heavy black horse holding pentacle; ploughed field behind.
- 76 Queen: queen on throne carved with goat heads and fruit in garden, holding pentacle in lap; roses above, rabbit at bottom right.
- 77 King: king on throne with bull heads, robe covered in grapes and vines, holding sceptre and pentacle; castle behind; foot on boar head.
