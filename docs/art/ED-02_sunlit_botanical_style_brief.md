# ED-02 Sunlit Botanical Herbarium — Art Style Brief (v2)

Reference composition & symbolism: Rider–Waite–Smith 1909 (public domain), https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rider%E2%80%93Waite_Tarot
Per-card content reference: `docs/art/RWS_1909_composition_reference.md`.

## Golden rule: FAITHFUL CONTENT, NEW RENDERING
- Card CONTENT must match the RWS 1909 original: same figures, poses, hands used, objects, symbol counts, positions (left/right), background elements, sky colour (e.g. yellow sky where original is yellow, black sky on Ten of Swords).
- **Do NOT add anything that is not in the original** — no extra suns/moons, no extra plants, flowers, vines, arbors, wreaths, animals or buildings. Do NOT remove or soften original elements (e.g. Ten of Swords: ten swords pierce the back of the prone figure; render without blood).
- Only the RENDERING changes: ED-02 engraving technique, paper, palette and border.

## Format
- Portrait 9:16, final size 768x1376 (normalized by `scripts/normalize_cards.py`), full-bleed card face.
- Thin double-rule sepia border, ~4% margin, small botanical corner flourishes (the ONLY place botanical decoration is added).
- **NO text, letters, numbers or Roman numerals anywhere** (name rendered by app UI). Where the original has inscriptions (High Priestess TORA, Wheel letters, Hierophant, etc.) replace with abstract engraved ornament of the same shape/position.

## Rendering style
- Fine-line botanical ink engraving (copperplate / herbarium plate look), cross-hatching & stipple shading.
- Paper: alabaster `#FAF9F6`, subtle grain. Ink: warm sepia/near-black `#1F2937`.
- Hand-tinted watercolor washes that follow the ORIGINAL card's colour placement, slightly muted toward the ED-02 palette (emerald `#059669`, sage, terracotta `#D97706`, ochre, dusty rose, slate blue).
- Plants that exist in the original (roses & lilies on Magician, sunflowers on Sun, lilies/hedge on Ace of Pentacles, vines on Nine of Pentacles, etc.) may be drawn with extra botanical precision — but never added where absent.
- Calm, editorial, archival; no glow, no neon, no 3D render.
